# Doseday arm recordings, 23 Oct 2026: data card

An SO-101 follower arm (`pi-arm1`, Viam) works a 7-day pill organizer (Sun..Sat lids in a row) and picks candy and Tums. There are ten recordings: teleop, hand-guided (manual mode) and scripted.

Labels are generated by `python scripts/label_runs.py`; rerun it after changing a curated event.

## Files in each run folder

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `README.md` | What happened, by hand |
| `motion.csv` (run 05: one `teleop-HHMMSS.csv` per try) | Raw recording, 6–9 rows/s |
| `*_labeled.csv` | Raw columns plus `tip_x tip_y tip_z`, `box_behind box_along_off box_above nearest_day`, `phase` |
| `labels.csv` | Segments: `file, t_start, t_end, phase, target, outcome, evidence, notes` |
| `frames/`, `frames_index*.csv` | Wrist camera at 1/s, each frame mapped to a motion time and a phase |

## Columns and units

- `t` is seconds since the recording started.
- `L0-L4` are leader joints, `F0-F4` follower joints, in degrees.
- `jaw_pct` is the follower jaw: about 0.5% for an empty close, 2.6–9.8% holding candy, about 8% holding a Tums.
- `x y z` (mm) and `o_x o_y o_z theta`: **the arm's reported pose is the wrist-roll joint, not the jaw tips.** The SO-101 kinematics in Viam has no tool offset.
- `tip_x tip_y tip_z` (mm) is the **jaw grasp point**: 98 mm further along the claw axis, from `box.Kinematics().tip(joints)`. Our forward kinematics matches the arm's reported poses to a median 0.3 mm. Use `tip_*` for anything about where the jaws are.
- `load0-4` is filled only in manual mode, and saturates at the manual torque limit (30), so it can't sense contact.
- **Box frame** (`box.demo_box()`), runs 04–06 and exploration b/c only:
  - `box_behind`: mm beyond the lid's front edge (away from the arm)
  - `box_along_off`: mm from the nearest lid centre along the box
  - `box_above`: mm above the closed lid top
  - fitted from Alex's run-04 presses: lid pitch 32.7 mm, box 6.2° off square, Wednesday straight ahead
  - blank for runs 01–03, the lid-button run and exploration a, where the box sat elsewhere or moved mid-run

## Frames

- Named `NNNN.Ns.jpg`, seconds since the camera started.
- `t_motion_est = t_frame - offset - 1.5`. The offset is per run (below); the 1.5 s is the camera's lag behind the arm, measured at 1–2 s.
- **Treat a frame's phase as ±1 s.**

## Labels

- **evidence `curated: ...`:** an event read from the README, the analysis or the frames, saying which. Outcomes appear only on curated rows.
- **evidence `auto: ...`:** a rule on the signals, filling the gaps:
  - jaw closing faster than 6%/s → `grasp_close`; opening → `release_open`
  - jaw-tip speed under 4 mm/s → `idle`
  - vertical speed below −10 / above 10 mm/s → `descend` / `lift`
  - otherwise `transit`
  - runs shorter than 0.6 s fold into the previous label
- **Phases:** idle, transit, survey_view, hover, descend, grasp_close, hold_transport, release_open, lift, lid_approach, lid_swing, lid_press, tab_press, manual_adjust, sweep.

## Known gaps

- **Tums identity:** a pill is called Tums only when the frame shows TUMS or it's the known demo pill.
- **Run 06 release:** a pill is visible in Friday afterwards, but the frames can't tell whether it's inside the compartment or on the half-open lid. The outcome is `unknown`.
- **Run 05:** Monday's outcome in try 4c was not checked.
- **Auto labels are a signal rule,** not ground truth. Trust curated rows first.

## 25 Oct 2026, Demo Day: pi-arm3 teleop recordings

A second Viam kit (`pi-arm3`, same SO-101 arms, its own calibration) at Luma Studios. Recorded with
`scripts/record_demo.py` while the black leader arm drove the white follower through Viam's teleop service.
Each folder holds `samples.csv` (one row per camera frame) and `gaps.csv` (reconnects); the wrist-camera frames
stay local like the 24 Oct ones.

Columns: `frame, unix_time, F0-F4, L0-L4, x y z, o_x o_y o_z theta, jaw_pct` and, from 16:01 on, `leader_jaw_pct`
(the black arm's grip). On this kit teleop mirrored only part of the leader's opening onto the follower
(`jaw_pct` peaked at 12% in `live-155817`), so `leader_jaw_pct` is the hand's intent and the replay uses it.
